How WaveCast Works

1. Permanent Fixed Room ID: Your room name is saved in your browser's localStorage and URL parameters. Whenever you reopen this page, your room name remains exactly the same.

2. Sharing Station Links: Click Copy Link to send a permanent URL like ?room=my-station. Listeners who open the link automatically tune into your station.

3. Rapid Refreshes & Mesh Conflict: If you refresh quickly while broadcasting, PeerJS cloud servers may take 2-4 seconds to release the old socket connection. WaveCast automatically detects unavailable-id and retries automatically.

4. Single Device Soundcheck: Click Soundcheck Synth to test the audio visualizer and peer mesh without microphone echo.
